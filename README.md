## 🧩 What's Inside?

### 1. 🔹 Phrasal Verbs (110+)
- Common phrasal verbs with **Bengali meanings**.
- Click on the Bengali text or the **pronunciation** button to hear the correct pronunciation.
- Perfect for building vocabulary and understanding idiomatic expressions.

### 2. 🔸 500 Verbs (3 Forms)
- A comprehensive list of verbs with their **V1, V2, and V3** forms.
- Each verb includes a **Bengali translation** and audio pronunciation.
- Ideal for mastering tenses and verb conjugations.

### 3. ⚡ Subject-Verb Agreement (33+ Questions)
- Carefully curated questions covering all major SVA rules.
- After selecting an answer, click **"Check Answer"** to see:
  - ✅ Correct / ❌ Wrong indication
  - 📘 A detailed **Bengali explanation** of the rule.
- Covers singular/plural subjects, collective nouns, indefinite pronouns, and more.

### 4. 📍 Prepositions (30+ Questions)
- Focuses on **appropriate prepositions** commonly tested in exams.
- Each question includes:
  - 🧠 A **memory formula** (e.g., `afraid of + thing`).
  - 📘 A **Bengali explanation** for the correct usage.
- Practice and solidify your understanding of prepositional phrases.

## 🖥️ How to Use

1.  **Download** the `index.html` file.
2.  **Open** it in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, etc.).
3.  **Navigate** using the colorful tabs at the top.
4.  **Click** on Bengali meanings or pronunciation buttons to hear the audio.
5.  **Attempt** the SVA and Preposition questions, then click "Check Answer" to learn from the feedback.

> 💡 **Tip:** Use the audio feature to improve your pronunciation and listening skills simultaneously!
